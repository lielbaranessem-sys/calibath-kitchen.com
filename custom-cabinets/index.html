Off-the-shelf options often lack the personal touch, leaving you with a look that feels generic and a layout that wastes valuable space.

That's why Cali Bath and Kitchen offers a different approach. Based in San Diego, CA, and proudly serving nearby areas including La Mesa, Carlsbad, Poway, El Cajon, Chula Vista, Del Mar, Coronado, and Fairbanks Ranch, we specialize in cabinetry that's tailored to your home. Storage Solutions Designed for Your Home and Lifestyle

Choosing the right built-in storage isn't just about selecting a look; it's about finding a team that understands how to merge everyday function with design and durability. At Cali Bath and Kitchen, storage and millwork services are tailored to suit kitchens, bathrooms, and full interior renovations, ensuring every upgrade feels like it truly belongs in your home.

Homeowners work side by side with our crew to create fully custom or semi-custom pieces that meet their exact requirements. That could mean squeezing extra organization into a compact galley kitchen, or crafting a vanity that feels at home in a spa-inspired bathroom. Because we focus on high-end remodeling, our woodwork blends seamlessly with flooring, tile, and other finishes for a unified result. We also provide style and color coordination so new pieces match your existing décor, or make the bold statement you've been envisioning. From wood species and hardware choices to surface finishes, every element is selected with care to complement your space.

Our in-house specialists handle demolition, measurements, fabrication, and installation. By keeping the entire process under one roof, we maintain quality control and a predictable timeline, no juggling outside vendors. We select strong materials, long-wearing finishes, and reliable hardware so your storage will stand the test of time. Because we only take on a handful of active projects at once, your build will have our full focus from start to finish. From the first sketch to the last hinge adjustment, our team creates storage that improves daily living and adds value for years to come. With Cali Bath and Kitchen, you're getting more than new cabinets; you're investing in functional beauty designed specifically for your home.
