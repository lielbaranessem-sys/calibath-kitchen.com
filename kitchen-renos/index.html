Built Around Today's Kitchen Design Trends

Today's kitchens are more than places to cook; they're central hubs for connection, conversation, and everyday living. Our clients are often looking to open up their space, improve flow, and create a kitchen that feels just as inviting as it is functional.

Many of the requests we receive include open concept & shelving, larger islands for prep and seating, and custom layouts that allow for easier movement and multitasking. Whether you're entertaining friends, helping with homework at the counter, or enjoying a quiet morning coffee, your kitchen should support your lifestyle.

Our design team keeps up with the most requested kitchen remodeling trends, including:

  • Spacious, open-concept designs
  • Smart storage solutions that reduce clutter
  • Warm, natural finishes like wood paired with white or neutral tones
  • Contemporary lighting and clean tile lines for a polished look

Kitchen Remodeling FAQs

Do I need to hire a separate designer or architect for my kitchen remodel?

No. Cali Bath and Kitchen offers a full-service design-and-build approach. Our in-house team handles everything from layout planning to finish selections, so you won't need to bring in outside designers or vendors.

Will I be working with the same people throughout my kitchen remodel?

Yes. Homeowners work directly with our project manager and crew leaders, not through layers of salespeople or subcontractors. We limit the number of active projects at a time to ensure clear communication and dedicated attention.

What parts of the kitchen remodel does your crew handle?

Our professional crew covers demolition, plumbing, electrical, tile, waterproofing, cabinetry, flooring, and painting. Every trade is handled by trained specialists on our team, not outsourced to third parties.

Do you help with permits for kitchen remodeling in San Diego?

Yes. If your project requires permits, we handle the entire process, from drawings to city approvals, so you don't have to manage it yourself.

How often will I get updates during construction for a kitchen remodel?

Our project managers visit your home daily and provide regular updates on the progress, what's been completed, and what's coming next. We keep you fully informed every step of the way.
