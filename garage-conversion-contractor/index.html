Benefits of a Garage Conversion

From Garage Space to Income-Generating ADU

Many homeowners are transforming their garages into fully functional Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) to generate extra income or provide comfortable accommodations for family members. As experienced ADU specialists, we guide you through every step of the process, from interpreting state and local regulations to designing a layout that maximizes your space and complies with all legal requirements.

We can help you create:

  • Studio ADUs : Compact, efficient layouts ideal for short-term rentals or guest stays.
  • One-Bedroom ADUs : Comfortable, private living spaces perfect for long-term tenants or extended family.
  • Luxury ADUs: High-end finishes, premium fixtures, and designer touches for an elevated living experience.

With recent changes in California law, converting a garage into a legal ADU has never been more attainable.
