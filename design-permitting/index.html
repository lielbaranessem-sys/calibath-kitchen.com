Our Full-Service Approach

Unlike other remodeling companies that require you to find your own architect or permit specialist, Cali Bath and Kitchen provides everything under one roof. We manage the entire process, ensuring efficiency, compliance, and high-quality craftsmanship every step of the way.

1. Initial Consultation & Project Evaluation

The first step is understanding your goals. During our free in-home consultation, we'll discuss:

  • Your vision, style, and functionality needs
  • Budget considerations and project feasibility
  • Structural and zoning requirements
  • Any HOA or city-specific restrictions in San Diego

This is where we determine whether your project requires specific permits, zoning variances, or engineering plans before proceeding.

2. Custom Design & Architectural Planning

Once we define your vision, our expert team of designers and architects creates a custom design that aligns with your needs and budget. We provide:

  • 3D renderings and digital plans to help you visualize the final result
  • Architectural drawings that meet your City & County building regulations
  • Structural calculations and engineering plans (if needed)
  • Material selection guidance to ensure durability and aesthetic appeal

Our goal is to design a space that is both beautiful and functional while staying compliant with local building codes.

3. Permit Acquisition & City Approvals

San Diego has strict permitting requirements, and securing approvals can be complex. With our team handling the process, you won't have to worry about paperwork or delays. We take care of:

  • Submitting all necessary applications to the San Diego Building Department
  • Handling zoning, occupancy, and environmental reviews
  • Expediting permits to avoid unnecessary wait times
  • Addressing any feedback or required modifications from the city

By working directly with city officials and inspectors, we ensure your project is fully compliant and ready for construction without setbacks.

4. Engineering & Structural Requirements

Some projects, especially room additions, wall removals, and structural changes, require engineering approvals. Our licensed structural engineers review every detail to ensure:

  • Load-bearing walls are properly assessed before modifications
  • Foundation and framing adjustments meet safety standards
  • Electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems are planned correctly

With our in-house team, you won't have to hire a separate engineer; we take care of everything.

5. Final Approval & Construction Readiness

Once all designs are finalized and permits are approved, your project is ready for construction. At this stage, we:

  • Provide you with a detailed timeline and scope of work
  • Ensure all permit requirements are met before breaking ground
  • Coordinate with our construction team to begin work flawlessly

With all the necessary approvals in place, you can have peace of mind knowing your project is fully compliant, structurally sound, and ready to be built.
