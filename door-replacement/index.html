Is It Time for New Doors? Here's What to Know

Replacing your doors can instantly improve security, comfort, and curb appeal, but like any home upgrade, it's important to weigh both the benefits and the potential challenges. Here's what you should consider:

Explore Different Types of Doors and Materials

Every door serves a purpose, and the right material or style can make all the difference.

  • Metal Doors Known for strength and durability, ideal for exterior security.
  • Wood Doors Timeless style with natural warmth, available in many finishes.
  • Glass Doors Brighten interiors and create open, airy spaces.
  • Sliding Doors Great for patios and backyards, offering easy outdoor access.
  • Accordion Doors Expand living spaces with wide openings and modern appeal.
  • Interior Doors Provide privacy, sound control, and design flow within the home.
  • Exterior Doors Built to resist weather while boosting curb appeal and safety.
  • Hollow Core Doors Lightweight, affordable, and practical for many rooms.
  • Solid Doors Heavier, more durable, and excellent for insulation and soundproofing.
  • How do I know if my doors need to be replaced? Signs include drafts, difficulty opening or closing, visible damage, or rising energy bills. Cali Bath and Kitchen can inspect your doors and recommend the best solutions.
  • How long does a door replacement project take? Most door replacements can be completed in a single day, though larger projects like multiple doors or patio door systems may take longer. We provide clear timelines upfront.
  • What types of doors can you install? We offer a wide selection, including entry doors, sliding patio doors, French doors, interior doors, exterior doors, accordion doors, and storm or security doors. Our design team helps you choose the best option for your home.
