Our Process

We believe in a collaborative, transparent approach to remodeling. From the initial consultation to the final reveal, we work closely with you every step of the way. Here's how our process works:

  • Consultation & Design: We start with a detailed consultation, during which we listen to your ideas, preferences, and goals for your home remodeling. Our design team will then work with you to create a customized plan that reflects your vision while also considering budget, functionality, timing, and local building codes.
  • Planning & Permits: Once we have a design in place, we'll begin the planning phase. We'll help you navigate the permitting process and handle all the necessary paperwork to ensure your remodel is fully compliant with local regulations and building codes.
  • Construction: Our experienced team will begin the construction phase, updating you daily on progress and addressing any concerns that may arise. We pride ourselves on quality craftsmanship, management, and attention to detail, ensuring that every element of your remodel is completed to the highest standards.
  • Final Touches & Clean-Up: Once construction is complete, we'll add the final touches to your remodel, including paint and flooring. We'll also handle the clean-up, leaving your home looking brand new and ready to enjoy.

5.Inspection & Walk-Through: Once the project wraps up, we dive into a meticulous inspection to guarantee flawless functionality. Then, we guide you through your transformed space, ensuring it exceeds your expectations and leaves you thrilled with the results.

Whole-Home Remodel FAQs

How long does a full home remodel take?

The timeline for a full home remodel typically ranges from 8 to 16 weeks, depending on the home's size and project scope. From demolition to final touches, we manage every step efficiently. While permitting and design might add a few initial weeks, rest assured you'll receive a clear schedule before construction kicks off.

Will I need to move out during the whole-home remodel?

In some cases, yes. Especially when remodeling crucial areas like bathrooms or the kitchen. For safety and comfort, many clients opt to stay elsewhere during major phases like demolition or plumbing. If you decide to remain at home, we'll assist in planning around the work to keep disruptions minimal.

What if I only want to remodel part of my home?

That's completely fine. While our expertise lies in full interior transformations, we're equally adept at kitchen makeovers, bathroom overhauls, expanding rooms, and converting garages or attics. If you're not ready for a complete renovation all at once, we can craft a phased plan to suit your pace.

Do you help with permits and inspections for a whole-home remodel?

Of course! We take charge of the entire permitting process. Our expert team crafts and submits plans, liaises with city officials, and guarantees your project aligns with local building codes. Plus, we handle inspections to ensure your remodel stays on schedule and compliant.

Can you remodel older homes with outdated wiring or plumbing?

Absolutely! Our skilled team excels at transforming homes with aged infrastructure. We expertly modernize electrical systems, replace outdated plumbing, and ensure everything meets today's safety standards. If hidden issues arise, we guide you through the solutions to guarantee a flawless fix.
