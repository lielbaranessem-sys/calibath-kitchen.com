What to Expect with Solar Panel Installation

Installing solar panels is a smart way to save on energy costs and increase your home's value, but like any major project, it comes with both benefits and considerations. To help you make an informed decision, here's what you should know:

  • How long does a solar panel installation take? Once permits are approved, most solar panel installations take 1, 3 days. Cali Bath and Kitchen provides a clear schedule and keeps you updated at every stage.
  • Do I need permits for solar panel installation? Yes, permits are required to ensure safety and compliance with building codes. Cali Bath and Kitchen handles the entire permitting and inspection process, so you don't have to deal with paperwork or delays.
  • How much can I save on energy bills with solar panels? Savings vary depending on your energy use, roof size, and system design. Many homeowners see significant reductions in their monthly bills. Our team designs systems that maximize your savings.
  • Will solar panels work on my roof? Most roofs can support solar panels, but factors like shading, orientation, and roof condition affect performance. Cali Bath and Kitchen evaluates your roof during the consultation to ensure the best setup.
  • How long do solar panels last? Solar panels are built to last 25, 30 years or more with minimal maintenance. Cali Bath and Kitchen installs high-quality panels backed by warranties, giving you peace of mind and long-term performance.
