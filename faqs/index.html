Your Remodeling Questions, Answered

WHAT MAKES CALI BATH & KITCHEN DIFFERENT FROM A PAPER GENERAL CONTRACTOR?

Unlike a paper general contractor who mainly coordinates paperwork and subcontractors, we actively manage every project in person. We perform regular site visits, supervise trades, inspect workmanship, ensure code compliance, and stay involved until your project is completed to the highest standards and your complete satisfaction.

CAN A GENERAL CONTRACTOR DO MOLD REMEDIATION?

Yes, a general contractor can do mold remediation, but it is important to ensure that they have the necessary training and certification to handle this type of work safely and effectively. Mold remediation involves identifying and removing mold from a property, as well as taking steps to prevent its recurrence.

CAN GENERAL CONTRACTORS DO ELECTRICAL WORK?

Absolutely! General contractors can perform electrical work, but the extent of their electrical work may be limited depending on the state and local regulations governing their profession.

CAN A GENERAL CONTRACTOR DO ROOFING?

Yes, a general contractor can do roofing as long as they hold the appropriate license and have the necessary expertise. However, it's important to note that not all general contractors specialize in roofing, so it's important to research their experience and qualifications before hiring them for a roofing project.
